The Artist

An individual voice in classical music.

Kristina Izgieva is a concert pianist and educator whose musical journey connects the traditions of the Saint Petersburg Conservatory and the Buchmann-Mehta School of Music at Tel Aviv University.

Her artistic life encompasses solo performance, chamber music and creative collaboration, alongside an enduring dedication to teaching.
